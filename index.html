Our Story

More than a café —
a way of life.

Peter was born from a simple belief: that a great cup of coffee can change the course of a day. Founded in 2014 by Peter Callahan, our café began as a tiny corner roastery with a hand-cranked drum and a dream.

Today, we source single-origin beans directly from farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — building relationships that go beyond trade and into genuine partnership.
